Plain-language policy

Your video stays yours.

Dialog Only Switch runs entirely in your browser. We do not upload your video, captions, classifications, or practice activity.

What is kept on this device

Your most recent WebVTT text, file name, filter choice, cue corrections, and practice completions may be saved in browser storage so the session survives a refresh. Local video files are never saved by the app and must be selected again.

What leaves this device

Nothing from the viewer. This version has no accounts, analytics, advertising, trackers, or third-party runtime requests.

Your controls

Use “Export session” to download your saved caption settings, or “Clear saved session” to remove them. Clearing site data in your browser also removes everything.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site operator through sociobot.in. Effective 28 August 2026.
