Plain-language policy

Your video stays yours.

Dialog Only Switch runs in your browser. We do not upload your video, captions, cue labels, or practice activity.

What is kept on this device

Your latest WebVTT text, file name, filter, cue changes, and practice results may be saved in browser storage.

This saved session can survive a refresh. Local video files are never saved and must be selected again.

What leaves this device

Nothing from the viewer. This version has no accounts, analytics, advertising, trackers, or third-party runtime requests.

Your controls

Use “Export session” to download your settings. Use “Clear saved session” to remove them from this browser.
